Academics

A Curriculum with Many Doors

One comprehensive system, two components — domestic and international — now with a flagship pathway to America’s universities.

Curriculum System

Two Components, One System

The international component is anchored by a new US pathway — and continues to provide established study routes to South Korea, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia and beyond. The domestic component comprises Gaokao preparatory classes — a pure academic track and an arts-and-sports track — a 3+3 integrated pathway, and a Joint Entrance Examination preparatory class for students from Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan.

01

US Pathway Program

Boya is expanding its international curriculum toward American higher education. Building on the Liberal Arts & Sciences track, the US pathway pairs a rigorous academic foundation — languages, mathematics, natural sciences and social sciences — with progressive English-language development and individual academic planning from day one.

Students receive one-on-one overseas-study counseling covering course selection, standardized-test planning (TOEFL, SAT), activity development and end-to-end application support, so that each graduate can present a coherent, competitive profile to universities in the United States and other English-speaking destinations.

Boya International Academy is accredited by Cognia, a globally recognized accrediting body for K-12 schools, and reviews its programs against international standards on a continuing basis. Course details for the current academic year are available from the admissions office.

02

Domestic Gaokao Program

Teaching delivery fully complies with the Shanghai Regular Senior High School Curriculum Implementation Plan. The program covers all compulsory subjects — Chinese, Mathematics, English, Politics, History, Geography, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Physical Education and Information Technology — with flexible arts and science elective pathways.

All students complete the Senior High School Proficiency Qualification Examinations before the end of Grade 11. Two university admission tracks are accessible in Grade 12: the Shanghai Spring College Entrance Examination in the first semester, and the National Unified College Entrance Examination the following June — offering diversified routes to target undergraduate institutions.

03

Arts & Sports Gaokao Program

This admission track encompasses two major categories: Arts — fine arts, performance and directing, music, dance, broadcasting and hosting — and Sports, including football, basketball, track and field, taekwondo, martial arts and sanda.

For arts majors, professional training is scheduled on weekends and during intensive winter and summer sessions, while weekdays are devoted to academic coursework: a solid academic foundation in Grade 10, specialized skill enhancement in Grade 11, and intensive mock examinations in Grade 12 leading to the Shanghai Municipal Unified Arts Examination. For the Shanghai Gaokao, the comprehensive score for arts majors is calculated as Arts Specialty Score × 1.1 + Academic Course Score × 0.9 (subject to the official announcement of the Shanghai Municipal Education Examination Authority for each academic year).

Sports majors may choose between Separate Sports Enrollment (specialty score weighted 0.7, academic 0.3, with national unified papers in Chinese, Mathematics, English and Politics) and Unified Sports Enrollment, whose weighting follows the municipal admissions regulations of the corresponding year.

04

3+3 Integrated Pathway Program

Designed for students who hold Shanghai household registration, this program leads from the three-year senior high curriculum directly to full-time junior college through an independent admissions process. The junior college diploma is officially verifiable on the China Higher Education Student Information and Career Center (CHSI) platform.

During the junior college period, students may pursue a bachelor’s degree through the college-to-university upgrade examination, or choose to enlist in military service as student soldiers.

05

Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Class — Hong Kong, Macao & Taiwan

This preparatory class is designed for students holding residency status in Hong Kong, Macao or Taiwan. The program strictly follows the national Joint Entrance Examination syllabus, with an experienced faculty providing systematic instruction on core test points, intensive practice with past examination papers, and tiered, individualized tutoring.

The curriculum balances foundational liberal arts and sciences with the development of arts and sports specialties. Personalized academic planning and application guidance accompany students throughout the preparatory journey, supporting them in competing for admission to prestigious higher education institutions in mainland China.

Signature Program · 06

Korea Pathway Program

Boya International Academy has established official cooperation with prestigious Korean universities — including Yonsei University, Korea University and Pukyong National University — building a stable, well-supported undergraduate pathway for its students. All partner degrees are recognized by the Chinese Ministry of Education.

How the Pathway Works

The program integrates Korean language, Korean culture and the standard high-school curriculum, delivered through a collaborative teaching model featuring both Chinese and Korean instructors. Students who pass the school’s internal comprehensive assessment and meet Korean language proficiency requirements are eligible for admission consideration by partner universities — without additional overseas entrance examinations.

One-stop support covers academic planning, visa processing and on-site guidance abroad. Graduates may benefit from policies for returning students, such as household registration, civil service eligibility and employment with multinational corporations.

Featured Course

Yonsei Korean Language Institute Courses

The Yonsei Korean Language Institute course is the Institute’s official curriculum, and the designated Korean course of the joint pre-admission training program with Yonsei’s arts college. It follows a dual-teacher model: online lectures by the Institute’s own Korean-language faculty, with on-campus Korean teachers providing in-person support.

Built on Yonsei University’s official curriculum framework and benchmarked to the standards of its home campus, the course lets students learn from professional Korean instructors without leaving Shanghai.
